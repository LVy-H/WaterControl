    Model and safety limits

    This is software-in-the-loop simulation using the firmware's C++ controller. Tanks, flows, hydraulics and the 450 pulses/litre calibration are illustrative. Blue pipes, pump symbols and valve symbols show controller commands, not physical feedback. Delivery values are model truth, not installed sensors.

    A full tank or open float wire removes permission. A leak after a branch meter can remain undetected. Low flow does not prove blockage. Simulation does not validate wiring, pressure, motor protection, valve travel, or real-world safety.

    Only actions advance virtual time. Multiple tabs share one engine. The simulated cloud does not contact Blynk. Fault acknowledgement clears a latch without repairing the cause or restarting a pump.
